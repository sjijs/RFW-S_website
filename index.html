Read the abstract ＋

Everyday deformable objects require both broad semantic coverage and physical configurations that can be tested under interaction. We introduce RobotFlyWheel-Soft (RFW-S), a dataset of 100,000 generated assets spanning 485 everyday categories across soft solids, cloth sheets, shells, garments, and bags. Construction connects reference appearances and geometry to structure-aware representations, executable simulator parameters, calibration feedback, and independent testing of the selected configuration. The intersection of physical verification and runtime checks retains 83.4% of the pool and all categories. In an expanded bag experiment, an independent stimulus rejects 3.87% of candidate evaluations that satisfy calibration, demonstrating the additional screening provided by testing beyond the fitted interaction. Garment comparisons characterize the effects of combined material and structural settings; a grasping application separates task completion from a deformation-derived integrity criterion. RFW-S associates semantic coverage with explicit configuration and behavioral evidence, supporting reproducible deformable-object studies within the documented solver and interaction scope.

TASK + INTEGRITY

Did it finish the task?
Did it satisfy the integrity criterion?

RFW-S reports these outcomes separately. Intact success requires task completion and a deformation-derived damage score below 0.25.

The damage score is a postprocessed deformation criterion; it does not measure physical cracks or coupled fracture dynamics.

The simulation uses prescribed motion and an approximate damage model; this visual comparison does not establish quantitative sim-to-real transfer accuracy.
